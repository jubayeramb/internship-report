\section{Introduction}

\par WeCycle \cite{wecycle2024} is a modern junk removal service provider company. The process WeCycle operates is by taking bookings from the users via WyCycle website then find haulers to do the job. We are connecting the local hauler with the users using this platform. Benefiting both side also we are helping our environment clear using our service. WeCycle regularly post blogs on mass awareness for our environment.

\section{Company Profile}

\par \textbf{WeCycle} (a product of \textbf{Tanbel Inc.}) is bridging the gap between the users and the hauler. Most users do not have enough information about their local haulers but we do. As the local hauler does not have strong present online most people get a hard time finding one. We currently operate in USA and our team is divided in three main parts which are development, design and marketing team. We are making the junk removal process modern and widely accessible to the internet.

\section{Organizational Structure}

\par WeCycle has a flat structure. Everyone can share their opinion. The team works as a whole person instead of a hierarchical structure. This helps us to involve in business decision and overall betterment of our company.
\subsection{Type of Organization}
\par WeCycle is a startup. It has only 10 member on the team. We works based on small wins rather than planning a whole year of work. We take review from users and the team then decide what to do this week based on that we work and ship.

\subsection{Interactions with Various Departments}
\par First our work start from collective decision made my the team what to do and our founder approve the plan. We give our opinion on plan approvement and then the designer team make a design based on approved plan. After that me and the designer work together to bring the design into code. The designer team review the the design reflected by the code and the development team review my code for best practices, maintainability, proper abstraction and reusability check. More importantly the feature is correctly implemented in proper way. The feature go the the staging server where everyone test the feature and report potential bugs and I need to fix them. After the the feature is shipped for marketing. Sometimes, marketing teams need something or change something, they ask me to do that. I work on it and ship it to the production.

\subsection{Collaboration with Team Leads}
\par The team lead firstly brief the feature and explain the plan how the feature should be implemented. Then I start working by following the plan. The process is not fully smooth, I need to get back to the team lead couple of times for clarification or modification of plan based on challenges we face during development. After the implementation is done, the team lead throughly review the code and ask more improvements after that the code it merged.

\subsection{Cross-Functional Coordination}
\par The development process is very dynamic. We use Jira for this process to be trackable and to manage and store the code we use git and github. We use google meet for discussions and use slack for communication.

\section{Products/Services}

\subsection{WeCycle Dashboard Application (WCAPP)}
\par The WyCycle dashbaord is the main source of our income. We provide a subscription based plan for the haulers where haulers can create accounts and get jobs from us. We also provide a proper \textbf{CMS} tool for hauler to manage their bookings, create invoices, manage crews, list bookings on the calendar etc.

Key features I worked with:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Lead Management:} Hauler can create lead information based on their findings or WeCycle provide hauler the lead to work with.
    \item \textbf{Client Management:} The Client are the leads which had been successfully converted. Hualer can store their information in future use.
    \item \textbf{Job Scheduling:} We provide a proper job management system where hauler can create their own jobs or they will get jobs from WeCyle. The can search and filter based on the requirement.
    \item \textbf{Crew Management:} Hauler can create a crew and give permission based access to other memebers. Creating, managing crews in one place.
    \item \textbf{Invoicing:} Hauler can easily create a invoice based on customer requirement and sent it to the customer. Giving a quote is very important to avoid any misconceptions.
    \item \textbf{Team Chat:} We have a build in Chat system where hauler can communicate with their team and the customers.
\end{itemize}
