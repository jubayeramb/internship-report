\par Program Outcomes (POs) are the necessary requirements to evaluate the technical, professional skills, ethical competencies knowledge gained in the course of my B.Sc. in.
Introduction to Computer Science and Engineering.
Academic coursework focuses on theoretical backgrounds; however, Program Outcomes make sure that graduates can put this into practice knowledge in professional and
industrial settings. When I was on industrial training, I was able to use CSE knowledge
in real-life tasks of backend development, including. as debugging, testing, cooperation, documentation and ethical decisions. This experience played a significant role in
closing the gap existing between the theory and the practice besides enhancing both the
technical and soft skills.

\par This mapping is not an academic formality. It is the evidence of transition:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Evaluating the appropriateness of academic curriculum to industry demands,
    \item Assessing the preparedness to implement the theoretical ideas with the help of
the latest tools and technologies, and
    \item  Being able to evaluate own development as an expert and moral being.
\end{itemize}
