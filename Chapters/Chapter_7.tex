\chapter*{Annexure: Mapping SDG Goals to Achievements from Industrial Training}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Annexure: SDG–Goal Mapping}

\par Most people think the UN's Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) \cite{sdg2015} are for governments. They're not. They're for engineers too. WeCycle is a waste management company. That means our entire business model is an SDG. We don't just write code; we build software that moves trash out of cities and into recycling centers. This isn't abstract for me. It's the code I wrote every day.

\par Engineering without responsibility is dangerous. Aligning my work with SDGs ensures I'm not just efficient, but also ethical. WeCycle uses tech to clean up the planet. By working there, I wasn't just fixing bugs; I was contributing (even in a small way) to cleaner cities and responsible consumption. It connects the "how" of coding with the "why" of engineering.

\par This mapping matters because:
\begin{itemize}
    \item It proves my code has real-world impact.
    \item It shows that "tech" and "sustainability" aren't opposites.
    \item It reminds me that engineering has consequences.
\end{itemize}
